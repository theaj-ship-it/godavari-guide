About the mission

One trusted starting point for pilgrims.

Godavari Guide is a proposed multilingual pilgrim assistance platform for Godavari Pushkaralu 2027. Its approach is to use an approved information database, clear search and AI-assisted matching to help people find relevant information without treating unverified chatbot responses as official facts.
